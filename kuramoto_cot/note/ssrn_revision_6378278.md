## Title
Synchronized Speculative Positioning in Futures Markets: A Pre-Registered Null and Exploratory Sub-Market Effects

## Affiliation
Université de Genève

## Abstract (plain text)
Coupled-oscillator (Kuramoto) measures are proposed as crowding indicators: when traders' position cycles align, synchronized exits should follow. We test this on CFTC leveraged-fund and managed-money positions in 32 futures markets with a causal phase and a pre-registered design. Pooled, the order parameter predicts neither returns, drawdowns nor volatility of the speculative book, in or out of sample, and its excess over independence is 89% ordinary comovement of positions. Exploratory splits tell a different story: in currencies, synchronization precedes deeper drawdowns of the leveraged-fund book beyond aggregate positioning (-0.24 s.d., out-of-sample R-squared of 6.8%), while in commodities it precedes continuation. We register held-out tests of both.
